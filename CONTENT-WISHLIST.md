# Content Wishlist — Materials to Gather for the Website
# 网站素材清单 — 需要收集的资料

Created 2026-08-27. Items marked ★ are the most important. Each item is given in English and Chinese so the Chinese text can be forwarded directly (e.g. via WeChat) to people at the factory.
创建于2026年8月27日。标★的是最重要的。每项均有中英文对照，中文部分可直接转发给工厂的同事。

## A. Company basics · 公司基本信息

1. ★ Exact founding year and key milestones (expansions, major equipment purchases, when partnerships with major customers began).
   ★ 公司确切的成立年份和发展大事记（厂房扩建、购置重要设备、与主要客户开始合作的年份等）。

2. Factory land area and workshop floor area (in square meters), number of workshops.
   厂区占地面积和车间建筑面积（平方米），以及车间数量。

3. Current total employee count, and how many are technicians/engineers.
   目前员工总数，其中技术人员/工程师人数。

4. Annual production capacity or output value figure that is OK to publish on the website.
   可以公开发布在网站上的年产能或年产值数据。

5. ★ Confirm the official English company name: "Rugao Binbin Machinery Manufacturing Co., Ltd."
   ★ 确认公司官方英文名称是否为 "Rugao Binbin Machinery Manufacturing Co., Ltd."。

## B. Capabilities & equipment · 加工能力与设备

6. ★ Complete equipment list: machine name, model, quantity, and key specs (table size, max turning diameter/length, max gear module for hobbing, etc.).
   ★ 完整设备清单：设备名称、型号、数量及主要参数（工作台尺寸、最大车削直径/长度、滚齿最大模数等）。

7. ★ Achievable precision: tolerance grades (e.g. IT6/IT7), surface roughness (Ra), and the maximum part size/weight you can machine.
   ★ 可达到的加工精度：公差等级（如IT6/IT7）、表面粗糙度（Ra值）、可加工的最大零件尺寸和重量。

8. Materials you commonly machine (carbon steel, alloy steel, stainless steel, cast iron, copper, nylon/engineering plastics, etc.).
   常加工的材料种类（碳钢、合金钢、不锈钢、铸铁、铜、尼龙/工程塑料等）。

9. ★ Quality inspection equipment list (CMM, hardness tester, roughness tester, etc.) and any quality certifications such as ISO 9001 — please provide photos/scans of certificates if available.
   ★ 质量检测设备清单（三坐标测量仪、硬度计、粗糙度仪等）及质量体系认证（如ISO 9001）——如有证书请提供照片或扫描件。

10. Typical lead times; whether you accept custom orders from drawings/samples; accepted drawing formats (CAD files, paper drawings); minimum order quantity if any.
    常规交货周期；是否接受来图来样定制；接受的图纸格式（CAD文件、纸质图纸）；是否有最低起订量。

11. Export / overseas shipping experience, if any.
    是否有出口或海外发货的经验。

## C. Customers & products · 客户与产品

12. ★ List of customers/partners that may be named publicly on the website (and their logos, if permitted).
    ★ 可以在网站上公开列出的客户/合作伙伴名单（如允许使用客户logo请一并提供）。

13. Names and uses of the main products in the photos — which machine/industry each part belongs to. (A photo list with questions will be sent separately for confirmation.)
    照片中主要产品的名称及用途——每种零件属于什么机器/行业。（稍后会另发一份照片清单逐一确认。）

14. ★ The original logo file (vector AI/EPS or high-resolution PNG) — the print shop that made the business cards should have it.
    ★ 公司logo的原始文件（矢量AI/EPS格式或高清PNG）——印制名片的印刷店应该有。

15. ★ A factory exterior photo taken on a clear day, and a photo of the entrance sign/nameplate. Note: the current exterior photo shows a sign reading "金龙机械厂" (Jinlong Machinery Factory) — is there signage with the Binbin name, or is it OK to show this sign on the website?
    ★ 晴天拍摄的厂房外观照片，以及大门招牌/铭牌的照片。注意：现有外观照片上的招牌是"金龙机械厂"——请问有没有"彬彬"名称的招牌？或者在网站上展示这个招牌是否合适？

16. ★ Bright workshop photos: turn on all lights, take wide shots of each section — CNC machining center area, lathe area, milling area, grinding area, and the gear hobbing machines (no gear hobbing photo yet).
    ★ 明亮的车间照片：请开启所有灯光，拍摄各区域的全景——加工中心区、车床区、铣床区、磨床区、滚齿机区（目前还没有滚齿机的照片）。

17. Photos of workers operating machines, and of quality inspection — measuring parts with micrometers, calipers, gauges.
    工人操作设备的照片，以及质检测量零件的照片（使用千分尺、卡尺、检具等）。

18. Packaging and shipping photos: parts being packed into crates, goods being loaded for delivery.
    包装发货的照片：零件装箱、装车发货的场景。

19. (Optional) Short video clips of machines running — could be used on the homepage.
    （可选）设备运行的短视频片段——可用于网站首页。

21. Whether you want a proper domain name + company email (e.g. info@binbinjx.com) instead of the QQ email — this can be arranged.
    是否需要注册正式域名和企业邮箱（如 info@binbinjx.com）替代QQ邮箱——可以协助办理。

22. Location/logistics info for the contact page: distance to the highway, to ports (Nantong/Shanghai), and to the airport.
    用于"联系我们"页面的位置与物流信息：距高速公路、港口（南通/上海）、机场的距离。
