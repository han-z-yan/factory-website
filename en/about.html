Company Profile

Rugao Binbin Machinery Manufacturing Co., Ltd. is located in the Yangtze River Delta, in the national-level economic development zone of Rugao, Jiangsu — a city known across China as the capital of flowers and bonsai and celebrated worldwide for the longevity of its residents. We are a manufacturing enterprise focused on precision machining.

Founded in May 1984, the company today has more than fifty engineers (management not included) with strong hands-on machining experience. We operate first-class production equipment — CNC machining centers, CNC lathes, grinders, milling machines, boring machines, and gear hobbing machines — supported by advanced production processes, a scientific management system, and thorough inspection. Guided by customer needs and committed to quality, the company has grown rapidly through technical progress and now produces at scale.

We operate two large production complexes: a manufacturing complex (floor area: TBD) and a processing & heat-treatment complex (floor area: TBD).

We machine a wide variety of mechanical components as a supporting manufacturer for well-known enterprises, with an annual output value in the tens of millions of RMB. Our integrity, capability, and product quality are recognized across the industry. Visitors are always welcome — come see the factory and discuss your project with us.

Our Story

  • May 1984 — Company founded in Rugao. The first machine arrived on the very night the founder's eldest daughter was born — the factory and the family have grown up together ever since.
  • Growth — Successive investment in CNC machining centers, CNC lathes, and gear hobbing machines built a complete turning–grinding–milling–boring–planing–drilling capability across two complexes: manufacturing, and processing/heat treatment. (Milestones: TBD)
  • Today — 50+ engineers (management not included), long-term supplier to leading machinery makers, annual output value in the tens of millions of RMB.
