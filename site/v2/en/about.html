Company profile

Rugao Binbin Machinery Manufacturing Co., Ltd. is a precision machining company in the national-level economic development zone of Rugao, Jiangsu, in the Yangtze River Delta. Rugao is known across China as the capital of flowers and bonsai, and worldwide for the longevity of its residents.

Founded in May 1984, the company has more than fifty engineers on staff (management not included), with deep hands-on machining experience. Production runs on CNC machining centers, CNC lathes, grinders, milling machines, boring machines and gear hobbing machines, under a disciplined management system with thorough inspection at every stage. Guided by customer needs and committed to quality, the company has grown into production at scale.

We operate two large complexes: a manufacturing complex (floor area: TBD) and a processing and heat-treatment complex (floor area: TBD). Annual output value is in the tens of millions of RMB. Visitors are always welcome.
