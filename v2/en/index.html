Industries we supply

Batch production of critical components for OEMs in three industries.

Printing machinery

Long-term supplier to printing machinery makers such as Jiangsu Changsheng Group: gripper-bar assemblies, shafts and gears.

Packaging machinery

Gears, bushings and precision shafts in batches, serialized piece by piece.

Mining machinery

Machined parts and large weldments: cutting, welding and finishing in-house.

Every part is traceable

Key parts are laser-etched or QR-coded piece by piece, and every batch travels with its process inspection card. If a part ever raises a question, we can trace it back to the batch, the operation and the inspection record.

Advanced processes, disciplined management and thorough inspection have kept this company standing for forty years. Quality first, integrity in business.
